About me

I'm an IT professional who gravitates toward the parts of the stack that keep things running — servers, pipelines, automation, APIs — but I'm comfortable shipping full-stack work when that's what the problem needs.

Most of my experience sits in backend and infrastructure: provisioning, troubleshooting production issues, scripting repetitive work away, and wiring up CI/CD so deploys aren't a ceremony. I like systems that are boring in the best way — predictable, observable, and easy for the next person to pick up.

I'm rebooting this site as a place to write about that work and what I'm learning next. If you're a fellow developer curious about how I think or what I've built, the blog and projects pages are the best starting points.

Currently exploring Roles where backend and infrastructure depth matter, with room to contribute on full-stack delivery. Writing here helps me sharpen that story.
